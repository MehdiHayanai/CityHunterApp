# CityHunter App: Video Demo Presentation

## The Intro (Presenter's Pitch)
> "Have you ever visited a place and forgot everything about it only a week later? Or worse—do you live in a city where you walk past the same monuments every day, yet know absolutely nothing about the history they hold or the secrets hidden in their architecture?
>
> We don't just have a memory problem; we have an engagement problem. We look, but we don't see.
> 
> That is why I built **CityHunter**. It’s not a map; it’s a mission. It’s an ecosystem that turns the street beneath your feet into a level in a game, and the monuments around you into conversation partners."

**Tagline:** "Wander with Purpose."  
**Core Concept:** Active Sightseeing where the city talks back.
